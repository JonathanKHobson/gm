Super Nintendo World RPG

Platform-game logic turned into a playable table loop.

A brief fan crossover campaign that translated power bars, coins, Stars, trophies, boss gates, unlockable characters, and custom sheets into tabletop play.

Fan crossover campaign Unlockable roster Boss-gated progression

What This Game Shows

Super Nintendo World RPG is a compact adaptation case. The craft is taking a familiar video-game language and turning it into goals, rewards, character choices, and pacing the table can understand.

The page is not a claim to own the characters or world. It shows how a known play fantasy can become a table-ready loop with clear sheets, visible progression, and character-specific moves.

  • Turns platform-game rewards into table goals and team decisions.
  • Uses custom sheets to make known characters playable in a shared RPG structure.
  • Builds progression through coins, Stars, trophies, boss gates, and unlocks.

What to notice

The character sheet translates a recognizable character into stats, abilities, and a playable action list.

Game craft

Adaptation that gives players a clear next move.

The design turns familiar platform-game feedback into tabletop procedures: collect, unlock, fight bosses, spend rewards, and bring new characters into play.

Table artifacts

The reward loop became visible.

The strongest proof is the table loop: goals players recognize, sheets they can read, and progression they can track without stopping play.

01

Super Smash bar

Every player tracked a power bar. Coins, Stars, trophies, and boss wins pushed characters toward a final special move.

02

Coins and team economy

Coins found through exploration, enemies, and puzzles became pooled choices for upgrades, feats, lives, items, and new characters.

03

Collectible objectives

Stars, Red Coins, Blue Coins, trophies, Super Smash Balls, and Chaos Emeralds gave the party visible goals beyond combat.

04

Castle gates

Castles worked as major boss fights gated by Stars, translating video-game progression into campaign pacing.

05

Unlockable roster

Players could rescue, complete levels, and defeat bosses to unlock new playable characters, then spend coins to use them.

06

Classless character builds

Each character had custom primary, secondary, bonus, attack, and Super Smash abilities instead of normal D&D classes.
