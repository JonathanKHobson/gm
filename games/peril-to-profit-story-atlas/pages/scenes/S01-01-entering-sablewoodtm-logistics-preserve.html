Act One · The Logistics Incident

S01-01 · Entering Sablewood™ Logistics Preserve

The carriage enters an ancient forest corridor rebranded as a safe logistics route.

Storyboard

The Old Sable is immense, living, and older than any corporate label. Goldspire has put its waymarkers into the ground, but the forest refuses to feel owned. Run this scene as threshold into branded wilderness and first moral test: The branded wilderness looks managed until the forest begins quietly disagreeing. Keep the camera close to practical choices, visible consequences, and the difference between ordinary people and the infrastructure pressing on them.

Player-Facing Read-Aloud

The road narrows between colossal trees. Goldspire waymarkers glow at measured intervals, promising safe passage, customer delight, and limited liability.

#rules-canon

Action Roll / Duality Dice

Roll two d12s, one Hope die and one Fear die. Add the fitting trait and any bonuses, then compare the total to the Difficulty.

Higher Hope die means with Hope. Higher Fear die means with Fear. Matching dice are a Critical Success.

Reaction rolls use the same dice structure but do not generate Hope/Fear or GM moves.

#rules-canon

Advantage, Disadvantage, Help, Experiences

Advantage adds a d6. Disadvantage subtracts a d6. They cancel one-for-one.

Help an Ally: an ally spends 1 Hope, rolls a d6, and adds it to the acting PC's roll.

Spend 1 Hope on a relevant Experience to add +2. Tag Team rolls cost 3 Hope and let two PCs act together.

#rules-canon + #p2p

GM Fear Economy and Fear Spends

GM starts with 1 Fear per PC and can hold up to 12 Fear.

Spend Fear to seize spotlight, make an extra move, spotlight extra adversaries, add adversary Experience, trigger Fear Features, advance countdowns, or reveal cost.

P2P Jenga Fear variant: with Fear means pull a numbered tower block. Block color is narrative corporate attention. Collapse fires banked consequences.

#rules-canon

Adversary Attacks, Damage, Armor, Stress

No fixed initiative. Pass spotlight by danger, intent, and consequences.

Adversary attacks roll d20 + attack against PC Evasion. PCs do not roll defense by default.

Damage uses thresholds. Armor Slots reduce severity by one threshold. If a PC is out of Stress and must mark more, they become Vulnerable and may mark HP.

#rules-canon + #adaptation

Short Rest, Countdown Die, Ritual Countdown

Short rest: each PC chooses two rest moves. The GM gains 1d4 Fear.

Countdowns are visible pressure. Tell players what changes when they tick.

Open Vale uses a ritual countdown. It ticks down when defenders make progress and up when the Custodian is harmed.

#rules-canon + #adaptation

Conditions

Core conditions: Hidden, Restrained, Vulnerable.

Goldspire conditions: Untethered, Audited, Bramble-Snared, Ward-Sick, Compliance-Flagged, Memory-Raw, Debt-Marked.

Describe the fiction first, then name the condition.
