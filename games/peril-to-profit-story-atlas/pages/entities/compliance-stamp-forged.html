Daggerheart Table Support

#rules-canon

Action Roll Flow

Ask what the PC does, choose the trait that matches the fictional approach, set or reveal the Difficulty, then roll.

Roll Duality Dice, add the trait and any applicable Experience or bonus, compare the total to the Difficulty, then read Hope/Fear spin.

Roll only when the outcome is uncertain and the consequence matters. If there is no meaningful pressure, give the result without a roll.

#rules-canon

Duality Dice

Official default: roll two d12s, one Hope die and one Fear die.

Total the dice, add the relevant trait and bonuses, then compare to Difficulty.

The higher die determines whether the roll is with Hope or with Fear. Matching dice are a Critical Success.

Reaction rolls use the Duality Dice structure, but they do not generate Hope or Fear.

#rules-canon

#rules-canon

Critical Success

When the Hope die and Fear die match, the roll is a Critical Success.

A Critical Success counts as a roll with Hope even if the total would otherwise miss the Difficulty.

On a Critical Success, the PC gains Hope and clears 1 Stress. On an attack roll, follow the official critical damage rules.

#gm-aid not official

D&D-to-Daggerheart Translation

D&D Conversion

Daggerheart uses traits, not D&D skills. Pick the trait that matches the player's fictional approach.

Use the D&D conversion page as a GM aid for players arriving from 5e; it is not official Daggerheart rules text.

#rules-canon

Advantage / Disadvantage

Advantage adds a d6 advantage die to the roll total.

Disadvantage subtracts a d6 disadvantage die from the roll total.

Advantage and disadvantage cancel each other out before rolling. If multiple advantage dice remain, use the highest one.

The GM may grant advantage or disadvantage when the fiction supports it.

#rules-canon

Experiences, Help, Tag Team

Experiences are short backstory/expertise phrases. Spend 1 Hope before a roll to add a relevant Experience modifier.

Help an Ally: spend 1 Hope, describe feasible help, roll a d6 advantage die, and add it to the acting PC's roll using the normal advantage rules.

Tag Team Roll: once per session, spend 3 Hope to combine two PCs' efforts. Both roll, choose one result for both, and resolve as a single action roll.

#rules-canon

Hope Uses / Fear Uses

Hope can be spent to Help an Ally, use Experiences, initiate a Tag Team Roll, or activate Hope Features.

On a roll with Fear, the GM gains Fear and makes a move reflecting the complication.

Fear can be spent to spotlight adversaries, use Fear Features, make stronger GM moves, apply adversary Experiences, and advance pressure when the rules or stat block call for it.

#rules-canon

GM Moves / Adversary Spotlight

GM MovesAdversary Spotlight

The spotlight moves through the fiction rather than a fixed initiative order.

The GM commonly makes moves when a PC rolls with Fear or fails an action roll.

Use softer moves to telegraph, reveal, or reposition. Use harder moves when the fiction and result call for direct consequence.

Spend Fear or use features when the adversary/environment rules call for it.

#rules-canon

Adversary Attack Rolls

Adversary Attack Rolls

Adversaries roll a d20 plus their attack modifier against the target PC's Evasion.

PCs do not roll defense by default. Use reaction rolls only when a rule, feature, or GM call gives one.

PC action rolls against adversaries use the adversary's Difficulty from the stat block.

#rules-canon

Damage / Armor / Stress

Damage / Armor

Compare incoming damage to the character's damage thresholds to determine how many HP are marked.

Mark an Armor Slot to reduce the severity of incoming damage by one threshold.

When a character marks their last Stress, they become Vulnerable until they clear at least 1 Stress.

If a character must mark Stress while all Stress slots are marked, follow the official overflow consequence from the character rules.

#rules-canon

Short Rest

A short rest is about an hour to catch breath and recover.

Each PC may swap domain cards between loadout and vault, then choose two moves, including choosing the same move twice.

Short rest moves: Tend to Wounds, Clear Stress, Repair Armor, or Prepare.

Prepare gains Hope; preparing with one or more party members gives each of those PCs 2 Hope.

#rules-canon + module tracker

Countdown

Countdowns are visible pressure clocks. Tell players what changes when a countdown ticks.

On progress countdowns, successful action rolls can tick the party closer to the goal.

On consequence countdowns, failures or success with Fear can tick the opposing pressure closer.

Open Vale uses a module ritual countdown as a tracker; do not treat it as a new condition.

#rules-canon

Ranges

Use Daggerheart range bands at the table: Melee, Very Close, Close, Far, Very Far, and Out of Range.

When exact distance is not important, keep ranges conversational and tied to reachable action.

#rules-canon

Official Conditions

Official Conditions

Daggerheart has three primary global conditions: Hidden, Restrained, and Vulnerable.

Some features create unique effects, but those are feature-specific text, not module-wide conditions.

Unless a rule says otherwise, the same condition cannot stack on one target.

Temporary conditions can be cleared with a fitting action roll, a GM-approved fictional method, or by an adversary spending its spotlight to clear the condition.

Unconscious appears only as Death Move/status context in this Atlas, not as a global condition card.
