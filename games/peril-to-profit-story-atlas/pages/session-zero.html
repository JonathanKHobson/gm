Quick Premise

The party begins as paid contractors or trusted associates working with Marlowe Fairwind and Emeris Crown Holdings to escort a sealed Keystone Asset through Sablewood™ Logistics Preserve to Hush.

Two-Minute Pitch

You are contractors in the Goldspire Territories, a corporate frontier where heroism is billable and safety comes with fine print. Marlowe Fairwind has a sealed delivery bound for Hush. The pay is real. The route is supposed to be safe. The job will not stay simple.

Character Selection / Customization

  • Fast one-shot: use pregens as written.
  • Guided pregen mode: rename, change pronouns/look/voice, adjust personality, choose a motive, and rewrite one Experience label only if mechanically equivalent.
  • Full custom mode: Level 1 characters only, with one Keystone Asset Custodian/responsible courier role preserved.
  • If nobody plays Marlowe, Marlowe becomes an NPC patron/contact or another PC takes the Keystone Asset Custodian function.

What PCs Know

  • They are delivering a sealed Keystone Asset.
  • Marlowe or another trusted custodian has authority over the job.
  • The route is supposed to be safe.
  • Payment depends on successful delivery.
  • They begin inside the system: loyal, ignorant, conflicted, pragmatic, or simply hired.

What PCs Do Not Know Yet

  • The cargo is a wardstone tied to the Goldspire Ward Network.
  • The route is already compromised.
  • Hush depends on fragile infrastructure.
  • The job is a symptom of broader corporate failure.

Tone and Safety Check

Corporate satire points at systems, not vulnerable communities. Hush is warm and sincere. Animal danger, body discovery, undead, memory pressure, and corporate exploitation can be softened, veiled, or paused without requiring explanation.

Safety Tools Placeholder

  • Lines and veils: hard nos and fade-to-black subjects.
  • X/N/O or equivalent: stop, slow down, okay.
  • Open Door: step away without explanation.
  • Buddy System and Not For Me, Thanks are available if Kyle wants them at the table.
  • Privacy note: players do not need to explain boundaries.

Intent Lines

  • I took this job because ___ .
  • The contract office thinks I am useful because ___ .
  • The part of the fine print I am ignoring is ___ .
  • The person I most want to impress on this job is ___ .

Corporate Relationship Prompts

  • Which corporation has harmed your family, and how do you talk about it?
  • Which corporate convenience do you secretly rely on?
  • What is the worst job you ever accepted because the pay was real?
  • What would make you break a contract in public?

                  Daggerheart Table Support

                  #rules-canon

                  Action Roll / Duality Dice

                  Roll two d12s, one Hope die and one Fear die. Add the fitting trait and any bonuses, then compare the total to the Difficulty.

                  Higher Hope die means with Hope. Higher Fear die means with Fear. Matching dice are a Critical Success.

                  Reaction rolls use the same dice structure but do not generate Hope/Fear or GM moves.

                  #rules-canon

                  Hope / Fear Outcome Matrix

                  TotalSpinResult
                  >= DifficultyHopeSuccess with Hope: succeed and gain Hope; add generosity or clean position.
                  >= DifficultyFearSuccess with Fear: succeed, but the GM gains Fear and makes the cost bite.
                  < DifficultyHopeFailure with Hope: miss the goal, but gain Hope and a useful thread.
                  < DifficultyFearFailure with Fear: fail and the GM makes a hard move that follows from the fiction.
                  matching diceCriticalCritical Success: best case, gain Hope, clear 1 Stress, and add a small gift.

                  #slider optional house layer

                  Slider Outcomes

                  Kyle's Slider System adds degree to the Daggerheart result. It is a house layer, not official rules text.

                  First read total against the hinge D. Then overlay Hope/Fear spin. Crit always takes the top outcome.

                  BandTotal vs DUse
                  Botch<= D - 5Failure and the situation worsens.
                  MissD - 4 to D - 1Failure with a partial thread or near miss.
                  HitD to D + 4Success at a cost.
                  CleanD + 5 to D + 9Clean success.
                  Soar>= D + 10Success plus extra leverage.
                  Critmatching diceTop outcome, gain Hope, clear 1 Stress, small gift.

                  #rules-canon

                  Difficulty Ladder

                  LabelDCUse
                  Easy10Low pressure, beginner-friendly, obvious route.
                  Medium / Standard15Default meaningful uncertainty.
                  Hard20Dangerous, contested, or specialized task.
                  Very Hard25Exceptional, unstable, rushed, or opposed.
                  Near-impossible30Legendary attempt; usually offer another route.

                  Traits and D&D-Brain Translation

                  TraitWhat it covers
                  AgilitySprint, leap, maneuver, dodge, balance, climb fast, keep footing.
                  StrengthLift, smash, grapple, hold, carry, resist force, endure strain.
                  FinesseControl, hide, tinker, pick, disable, handle delicate details.
                  InstinctPerceive, sense, navigate, track, read danger, read animals.
                  PresenceCharm, lead, soothe, deceive, perform, intimidate, negotiate.
                  KnowledgeRecall, analyze, investigate, comprehend, reason from lore.

                  #rules-canon

                  Advantage, Disadvantage, Help, Experiences

                  Advantage adds a d6. Disadvantage subtracts a d6. They cancel one-for-one.

                  Help an Ally: an ally spends 1 Hope, rolls a d6, and adds it to the acting PC's roll.

                  Spend 1 Hope on a relevant Experience to add +2. Tag Team rolls cost 3 Hope and let two PCs act together.

                  #rules-canon + #p2p

                  GM Fear Economy and Fear Spends

                  GM starts with 1 Fear per PC and can hold up to 12 Fear.

                  Spend Fear to seize spotlight, make an extra move, spotlight extra adversaries, add adversary Experience, trigger Fear Features, advance countdowns, or reveal cost.

                  P2P Jenga Fear variant: with Fear means pull a numbered tower block. Block color is narrative corporate attention. Collapse fires banked consequences.

                  #rules-canon

                  Adversary Attacks, Damage, Armor, Stress

                  No fixed initiative. Pass spotlight by danger, intent, and consequences.

                  Adversary attacks roll d20 + attack against PC Evasion. PCs do not roll defense by default.

                  Damage uses thresholds. Armor Slots reduce severity by one threshold. If a PC is out of Stress and must mark more, they become Vulnerable and may mark HP.

                  #rules-canon + #adaptation

                  Short Rest, Countdown Die, Ritual Countdown

                  Short rest: each PC chooses two rest moves. The GM gains 1d4 Fear.

                  Countdowns are visible pressure. Tell players what changes when they tick.

                  Open Vale uses a ritual countdown. It ticks down when defenders make progress and up when the Custodian is harmed.

                  #rules-canon + #adaptation

                  Conditions

                  Core conditions: Hidden, Restrained, Vulnerable.

                  Goldspire conditions: Untethered, Audited, Bramble-Snared, Ward-Sick, Compliance-Flagged, Memory-Raw, Debt-Marked.

                  Describe the fiction first, then name the condition.
