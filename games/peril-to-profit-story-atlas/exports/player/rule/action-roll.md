# Action Roll

Player Reference Card

## Hook
Roll when a PC does something uncertain and consequences matter.

## What It Is
Roll when a PC does something uncertain and consequences matter.

## How It Works
When a PC tries something uncertain and meaningful, choose a trait, set Difficulty, roll Hope d12 + Fear d12, add bonuses, compare total, then read Hope/Fear spin.

## Try It
Give a player Presence 12 to calm a worried animal, then ask what Hope or Fear changes in the scene.

## You Could
- Player states the action in the fiction.
- GM names the trait that best matches the method and the Difficulty.
- Player rolls Hope d12 + Fear d12 and adds trait, Experience, or advantage/disadvantage.
- Compare total to Difficulty, then resolve the Hope/Fear or critical result.
