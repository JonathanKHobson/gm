# Dungeon Workers' Alliance

Public Notice

## Hook
Dungeon Workers' Alliance: The Dungeon Workers' Alliance is an old labor name that survives in brass pins, hand-copied maps, and tired defiance. Slogan: Nobody clears the dungeon alone.

## Public Face
A half-legend of organized labor, old route pins, and workers who remember the dungeon never cleared itself.

## Slogan
Nobody clears the dungeon alone.

## How To Deal
The Alliance feels less like a banner and more like a promise passed hand to hand. A dull brass pin in a collar, a copied route map with names by the dangerous bends, a worker who says 'we' when the company file says 'incident.'

## You Could
- Ask what they publicly promise.
- Compare their slogan to what they actually do in the scene.
