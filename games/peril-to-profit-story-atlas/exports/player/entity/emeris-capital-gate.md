# Emeris Capital Gate

Goldspire Travel Advisory

## Hook
A great arch of pale carved stone three wagons wide, with toll-counters clacking beneath it and the green dark of Sablewood waiting beyond.

## Arrival Read
A great arch of pale carved stone rises three wagons wide, its keystone leafed in gold and its faces worn smooth by a century of carved caravans setting out. Beneath it runs a permanent little fair: toll-counters clacking, a crier calling the morning rates, a stall hawking...

## Known For
The western threshold of Emeris, route tolls, courier departures, gate charms, caravan queues, and the first straight look into Sablewood.

## What Is Here
Local residents or workers appropriate to the place horse-goats stamping in harness paperfin swifts nesting in arch nooks market beetles under food carts guide dogs sleeping in shade No default combatant; add only if the scene introduces one Emeris Crown...

## Food And Texture
Travelers buy fried dough, hot spiced cider, route tokens, painted gate charms, and advice from people who may or may not have survived the road themselves.
