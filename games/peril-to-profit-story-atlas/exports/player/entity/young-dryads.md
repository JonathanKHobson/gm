# Young Dryads

Field Guide Page

## Hook
Player-safe note: Small sapling spirits with bark-textured limbs, leaf hair, green-gold eyes, and shy defensive postures rooted around stones like children guarding a boundary.

## Observed Form
Childlike sapling spirits with bark limbs, leaf hair, glowing green-gold eyes, shy defensive posture, roots around stones, spring leaves, and moss.

## Behavior
Children of the wood with bark for skin, shy until a kindness is broken. They guard the stones like a held breath, and they only turn fierce when a guest takes the welcome they were given and spits on it.

## Warning Signs
Axes and fire, the Hunting Trees that were once kin gone wrong, and guests who smile and then steal.

## You Could
- Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
- Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
