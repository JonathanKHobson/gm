# Sablewood Route Gate

Goldspire Travel Advisory

## Hook
The road crossing where nature, signage, and corporate language first collide.

## Arrival Read
The route gate is where polished assurance meets wet bark, old stone, and a forest that has not signed anything.

## Known For
A tiny official station tries to draw straight lines across a forest that only does curves. Roots have buckled the queue-stones into a slow stone wave. Vines steal loose paper off the desk overnight. The inspection counter is a cut stump the size of a wagon...
