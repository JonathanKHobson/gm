# Badger-Hawks

Field Guide Page

## Hook
Player-safe note: Badger-hawks dive like feathered trouble, stealing catches off lines and sometimes trying to carry off the fisher's dignity with them.

## Observed Form
Give one concrete visual, one sound or texture, and one sign of who has used or neglected it.

## Behavior
Badger-hawks dive like feathered trouble, stealing catches off lines and sometimes trying to carry off the fisher's dignity with them.; show instinct first through distance, posture, scent, and how the creature protects what is close to it.

## Warning Signs
Being surrounded, misread as a monster, or driven toward The Refuge, The Ash Quarry, Sablewood Hybrid Fauna by noise and haste.

## You Could
- Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
- Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
