# Fire-Falcons

Field Guide Page

## Hook
Player-safe note: Sleek ember-orange falcons with coal-black wing tips and feather edges like banked fire, beautiful enough for prestige and sharp enough to make a cache feel watched.

## Observed Form
Sleek falcons with ember-orange plumage, coal-dark wing tips, glowing feather edges, and intelligent eyes perched near scorched Sablewood branches.

## Behavior
Beauty bred into a weapon. Banked-fire feathers the wealthy buy for spectacle and the Sinecure trains for harm. Loyal to whoever feeds them, sharp to everyone else, and aware, somewhere, that they are admired far more than they are loved.

## Warning Signs
Being chained for display, the smell of a stranger's fear, and losing their handler's voice in a crowd.

## You Could
- Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
- Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
