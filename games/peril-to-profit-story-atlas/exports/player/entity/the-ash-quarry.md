# The Ash Quarry

Goldspire Travel Advisory

## Hook
A quarry where ash has practical value, letting the setting show resource extraction without losing the strangeness of the old wood.

## Arrival Read
The Ash Quarry turns ancient residue into industry: gray terraces, warm dust, and workers who know which piles still remember fire.

## Food And Texture
Lunch is eaten gray-handed in a dusty shed, hard bread, salt pork, a tea that crunches faintly no matter how careful you are. The water is kept covered, and a clean cup is a small daily victory worth defending.
