# The Titan's Steps

Goldspire Travel Advisory

## Hook
A colossal pillar route rising more than a thousand feet through Sablewood, where merchants pay for altitude in sweat, rope, and hazard rate.

## Arrival Read
The Titan's Steps climb through height, rope, sweat, and hazard pay until travel itself feels like a negotiated risk.

## Known For
Each stair is the size of a town square, so a single step takes half a morning to climb. Grass and flowers have turned the lower treads into hanging meadows. A crack in the stone becomes a road you walk down the middle of. From the lip, the whole territory...
