# The Miremist

Goldspire Travel Advisory

## Hook
A mist-wrapped place where route certainty breaks down and the forest makes navigation feel like an audit of attention.

## Arrival Read
The Miremist makes certainty expensive: fog, wet roots, and route sounds that arrive from the wrong distance.

## Food And Texture
Almost nothing safe to eat grows out here, but the mire hides rare herbs worth a fortune to anyone brave or foolish enough to fetch them. Foragers carry their own bread and trust nothing they did not pack themselves.
