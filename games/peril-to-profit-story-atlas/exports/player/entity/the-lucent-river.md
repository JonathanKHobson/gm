# The Lucent River

Goldspire Travel Advisory

## Hook
A luminous river whose blue night glow lets travelers keep time and whose waters are sold, mythologized, healed by, and hunted in.

## Arrival Read
The Lucent River carries blue light through Sablewood like a road that refuses to become a road.

## Food And Texture
Ferry folk grill glass-fish caught straight from the bright water, sweet and faintly luminous, and sell river-cress by the handful. Pilgrims buy little bottles of the glow, which is mostly algae and entirely hope.
