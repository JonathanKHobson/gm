# Giraffe-Deer

Field Guide Page

## Hook
Player-safe note: Tall, soft-eyed giraffe-deer step over the carriage road as if the road is a small inconvenience built by very short people.

## Observed Form
Give one concrete visual, one sound or texture, and one sign of who has used or neglected it.

## Behavior
Tall, soft-eyed giraffe-deer step over the carriage road as if the road is a small inconvenience built by very short people.; show instinct first through distance, posture, scent, and how the creature protects what is close to it.

## Warning Signs
Being surrounded, misread as a monster, or driven toward Sablewood™ Logistics Preserve, Old Sable, Sablewood Route Network by noise and haste.

## You Could
- Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
- Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
