# Fox-Bats

Field Guide Page

## Hook
Player-safe note: Small fox-bats sleep under signs, steal shiny route tokens, and may tuck into a traveler's coat when the forest briefly feels kind.

## Observed Form
Give one concrete visual, one sound or texture, and one sign of who has used or neglected it.

## Behavior
Small fox-bats sleep under signs, steal shiny route tokens, and may tuck into a traveler's coat when the forest briefly feels kind.; show instinct first through distance, posture, scent, and how the creature protects what is close to it.

## Warning Signs
Being surrounded, misread as a monster, or driven toward Sablewood™ Logistics Preserve, Sablewood Hope Moments, Halython's Fox-Bat Companion by noise and haste.

## You Could
- Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
- Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
