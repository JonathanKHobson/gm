# Mountain Crabs

Field Guide Page

## Hook
Player-safe note: Granite-shelled cliff crabs with moss and alpine flowers growing on their backs, stone-heavy claws, many gripping legs, and wary eyes made for vertical roads.

## Observed Form
Huge stone-shelled crabs adapted to cliffs, granite armor, moss and small alpine flowers, many legs gripping rock, and beadlike wary eyes.

## Behavior
Grumpy granite tenants of the cliff with gardens growing on their own backs. Not malicious, just enormous and territorial on a road where one bad step is the last. They were here long before the climbing route and resent the traffic.

## Warning Signs
Falling, fire, and the vibration of too many boots drumming on their rock.

## You Could
- Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
- Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
