# Athervast

Goldspire Travel Advisory

## Hook
A city like a coiled crown, where gold-capped towers rise from the center and the rings spiral outward, lower and louder, until the gleam becomes rumor.

## Arrival Read
Athervast does not have a skyline so much as a spiral. At its heart stand grand pale towers capped in gold, so tall that clouds catch on them, and from that crown the whole city winds outward in vast circular tiers like the inside of a shell.

## Known For
Gold streets in the high rings, sky-illusions in the entertainment tiers, potion manufactories in the workrings, and the public claim that it is the safest and grandest city on Terra.
