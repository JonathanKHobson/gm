# Goldspire Logistics

Public Notice

## Hook
Goldspire Logistics: a dull freight mark, a grey seal, and a name too boring to remember.

## Public Face
A forgettable freight-coordination concern with plain grey seals and paperwork nobody remembers seeing twice.

## Slogan
Keeping everything moving.

## How To Deal
Goldspire Logistics does not look powerful. That is the trick of it. A grey seal appears on crate corners, approval slips, and route papers, always in the same modest ink, always small enough to miss if you are looking for a villain with a banner.

## You Could
- Ask what they publicly promise.
- Compare their slogan to what they actually do in the scene.
