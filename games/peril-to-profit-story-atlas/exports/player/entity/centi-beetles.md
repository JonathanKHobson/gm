# Centi-Beetles

Field Guide Page

## Hook
Player-safe note: Giant centi-beetles chew root and loam into low tunnels, making the ground sound busy even when nobody is walking.

## Observed Form
Give one concrete visual, one sound or texture, and one sign of who has used or neglected it.

## Behavior
Giant centi-beetles chew root and loam into low tunnels, making the ground sound busy even when nobody is walking.; show instinct first through distance, posture, scent, and how the creature protects what is close to it.

## Warning Signs
Being surrounded, misread as a monster, or driven toward Root's Hollow, Underroot, Underroot Tunnels by noise and haste.

## You Could
- Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
- Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
