## Hook
A walled city of honey-colored stone on the very lip of the great forest, where the last paved road in the world meets the first wild tree.

## Food And Texture
Emeris treats routes as gossip and safe returns as public celebration. Street food is fried festival dough, skewered river fish, and hot spiced cider sold at the gate to travelers heading into the trees.
