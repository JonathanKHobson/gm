# Halython's Fox-Bat Companion

Field Guide Page

## Hook
Player-safe note: A tiny russet fox-bat with huge ears, folded wings like a cloak, clever paws, and bright eyes that make Halython's card table feel watched by something playful.

## Observed Form
Foxlike face, oversized bat ears, delicate folded wings, tiny paws on a mossy branch, and a mischievous expression warmed by Sablewood light.

## Behavior
A pocket of curiosity with folded-cloak wings, perched where it can watch every hand at the card table. Bonded to Halython without being owned by anyone. Reads a room faster than most people and judges it kindly.

## Warning Signs
Being grabbed, caged, or treated as a party trick instead of a companion.

## You Could
- Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
- Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
