# Hunting Trees

Field Guide Page

## Hook
Player-safe note: Walking predatory trees with root legs, hooked branch arms, hollow amber eyes, and bark faces that make ordinary forest scenery feel like it might choose a target.

## Observed Form
Predatory walking trees with root legs lifting from the soil, hooked branch arms, moss, lichen, and hollow amber eyes in bark faces.

## Behavior
The point where the forest stops being scenery and starts being hungry. They do not hate; they harvest, the way a snare does. The horror is that they only move when you stop watching.

## Warning Signs
Fire, the cleared light of the open road, and prey that refuses to scatter.

## You Could
- Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
- Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
