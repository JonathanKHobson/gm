# Strixwolf Pup Two

Field Guide Page

## Hook
Player-safe note: A crouched strixwolf pup with darker back feathers, round eyes, and a body tucked near the roots as if the forest itself might hide it.

## Observed Form
Small crouched owl-wolf, dark feathered back, mottled face, tucked paws, and root-shadow posture.

## Behavior
The one that hides instead of freezing, wedged so far into the roots the forest seems to be keeping it. Darker, quieter, watching its sibling for the cue to bolt.

## Warning Signs
Being flushed out of cover, losing sight of its sibling, the cold gap where its mother should be.

## You Could
- Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
- Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
