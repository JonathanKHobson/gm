# Goldspire Territories

Goldspire Travel Advisory

## Hook
A vast bright frontier-fantasy territory of walled cities, giant forests, wet villages, relay towers, old roads, and cheerful safety promises that never quite cover the cracks.

## Arrival Read
Goldspire is not one city. It is a huge territory of paved capitals, painted forest roads, mossy villages, quarry towns, river markets, ridge relays, and old magic under newer paperwork.

## Known For
Gigantic forests, courier roads, territory capitals, branded protection, moss villages, bright markets, relay towers, and older magic that refuses to become a product.
