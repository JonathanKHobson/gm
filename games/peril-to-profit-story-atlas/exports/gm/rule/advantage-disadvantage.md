# Advantage / Disadvantage

GM export - rule:advantage-disadvantage

## What it is
Advantage adds a d6; disadvantage subtracts a d6.

## How it works
Use when the fiction gives a clear edge or obstacle.

## Step by step
- Use when the fiction gives a clear edge or obstacle.
- Roll a d6 and add it; disadvantage subtracts it.
- Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.
