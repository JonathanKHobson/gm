## What it is
Short expertise phrases that can add to relevant rolls by spending Hope.

## How it works
Use before a roll when the phrase truly applies.

## Step by step
- Use before a roll when the phrase truly applies.
- Yes, if it clearly helps this action and you spend Hope.
- Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.
