# Connection Prompts

GM export - rule:connection-prompts

## What it is
Short relationship questions that build party history with consent.

## How it works
Use before play to create one or two playable relationships between PCs.

## Step by step
- Use before play to create one or two playable relationships between PCs.
- One player offers a memory or relationship prompt; the other player answers from their character's point of view.
- Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.
