# Reaction Roll

GM export - rule:reaction-rolls

## What it is
A roll to react to danger or pressure rather than initiate action.

## How it works
Use when the GM calls for a response to sudden danger.

## Step by step
- Use when the GM calls for a response to sudden danger.
- Usually yes: if you mean a D&D save, look for a Reaction Roll.
- Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.
