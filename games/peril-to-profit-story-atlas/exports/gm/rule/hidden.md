## What it is
A target is unseen or its position is unknown.

## How it works
Use when the fiction supports being unseen or concealed.

## Step by step
- Use when the fiction supports being unseen or concealed.
- Enemies have a harder time targeting what they cannot see or locate.
- Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.
