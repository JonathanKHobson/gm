A PC's core playstyle, starting values, features, domains, and Hope Feature.

## How it works
Use when explaining what a pregen is good at or when a feature changes a scene.

## Step by step
- Use when explaining what a pregen is good at or when a feature changes a scene.
- It tells you your main features, starting Evasion, HP, domain access, and the kinds of actions your sheet supports well.
- Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.
