# Disposition / Resolution Track

GM export - rule:disposition-track

## What it is
A module-facing tracker for how a creature, NPC group, or faction leaves a scene: helped, calmed, fought, captured, spared, or harmed.

## How it works
Use for Strixwolf and Bramble-style outcomes that should ripple later.

## Step by step
- Use for Strixwolf and Bramble-style outcomes that should ripple later.
- Track what the table actually did and how the affected side now feels about them.
- Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.
