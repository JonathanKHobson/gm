# Goldspire Territories

## Player-facing description
Goldspire is not one city. It is a huge territory of paved capitals, painted forest roads, mossy villages, quarry towns, river markets, ridge relays, and old magic under newer paperwork.

## Public role
Run Goldspire Territories as the frontier and extraction hinterland outside Athervast: Emeris dispatches the one-shot job, Sablewood carries the road risk, Hush holds the immediate ward problem, and the faraway grand capital benefits from a safety economy it barely has to see.

## Handout text
