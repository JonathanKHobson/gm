Player-facing description

Emeris is honey-colored stone, crowded courier squares, fried gate dough, painted shingles, and the great western road leaning into Sablewood's green dark.

Public role

Emeris is the dispatch point, territory capital, and Sablewood-edge start city. It is safe and prosperous at the start; keep active ward crisis language in Hush scenes.

Handout text

Emeris: a bright territory capital at Sablewood's edge, where the last paved road becomes the job.

GM truth

Emeris is the one-shot start city and a separate territory capital. It begins publicly safe and prosperous; Hush, not Emeris, carries the immediate ward crisis.

GM handling

Use Emeris to ground the job in civic comfort before the table enters Sablewood; it is the launching point, not the reveal.
