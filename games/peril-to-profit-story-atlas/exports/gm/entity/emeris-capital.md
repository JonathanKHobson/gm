## GM truth
Emeris is the one-shot start city and a separate territory capital. It begins publicly safe and prosperous; Hush, not Emeris, carries the immediate ward crisis.

## GM handling
Use Emeris to ground the job in civic comfort before the table enters Sablewood; it is the launching point, not the reveal.
