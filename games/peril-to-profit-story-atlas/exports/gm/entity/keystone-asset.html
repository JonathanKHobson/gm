Keystone Asset™

GM export - entity:keystone-asset

Player-facing description

A massive lion-faced wardstone sealed as cargo. When exposed, it makes the air feel heavier, safer, and watched.

Public role

Keystone Asset™, Emeris Crown Holdings: the ward-stone that keeps Emeris safe. It is failing and must be renewed at Open Vale.

Handout text

Warning: sealed wardstone asset. Do not strike. Do not expose near unstable ward lines. Authorized keeper required for renewal.

GM truth

It is a master wardstone sent from Emeris toward Hush to renew failing protection. The wider truth is that outer ward failures can be monetized, not that Emeris or Athervast begins with an empty socket.

GM handling

Turn cargo into responsibility. The party starts paid to carry it; by Act Four they understand it protects communities and exposes the ward economy.
