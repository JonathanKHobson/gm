Player-facing description

A finely dressed woman waits beside a loaded carriage: worn leather coat, good boots, a book in one hand with a route-map tucked inside it. Her hair is a ragged mess. She carries herself like someone who's seen a fight and expects another.

GM truth

She is Sabine Quillon, Emeris Crown Holdings' field agent. Use her only as an optional S00-01 backup job-giver/contact. Reveal the name only when a PC asks or when she introduces herself in the played briefing.

GM handling

Use her as the optional question gate for Prologue names and as the first human crack in the corporate story. She is only needed for S00-01; after the handoff, she is offstage unless Kyle chooses otherwise.
