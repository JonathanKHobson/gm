## GM goal

## GM notes
- Hexmart Pocket Ward™ (cracked): No real ward effect; failed consumer protection charm. The driver trusted cheap protection and it failed.
- Stride Salve: Clear 1 Stress or move at Far without a roll once. Common frontier aid for exhausted couriers.
- Grindstone Vial: Next damage roll adds +1d6.
- Lark-Moth Lantern: Soft light; once/rest ask GM one question about immediate area, no roll.
- Bramble-Cutter's Hook: 1d6 physical tool-knife; advantage on Finesse or Strength rolls to cut ropes, vines, snares, or free someone Restrained by brambles.
- Compliance Stamp (forged): Once, auto-pass bureaucratic/checkpoint gate without rolling; risky if inspected.
- Firstmoss Poultice: Tend to Wounds: clear 1d4+1 HP outside a rest.
- Bag of Mixed Chits: Currency.
- Whetcord: Once, reroll a damage die and keep the higher.
- Tinker's Kit, Used: Advantage on Finesse rolls to pick locks, disarm simple traps, or jury-rig.
- Soul-Quiet Charm: One use: clear 1 Stress caused by fear, memory, or wraith backlash, or gain advantage on a Presence or Knowledge roll to recover from a Soul-Audit Wraith module effect. Adaptation, not official Daggerheart.
- Strixwolf-Down Cloak: Once/rest, advantage on Agility roll to move silently or break line of sight.
