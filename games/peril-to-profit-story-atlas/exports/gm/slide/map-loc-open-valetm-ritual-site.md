## GM goal
The climax. A perfect circular clearing — the only break in the canopy — where the Custodian first forged Hush's ward pillars. The party takes a Short Rest, then defends her through the ward-renewal ritual against waves of skeletons and Soul-Audit Wraiths while a countdown die ticks. Win condition: survive until the ritual completes, not "kill everything."

## GM notes
- Hub map: one place, multiple finale beats. Do not send players wandering into a new node.

## GM script
Say: "This is the finale hub. Use it for arrival, the Short Rest, the ritual start, the completion, and the transition to the report choice. Set the ritual countdown to 8. Each meaningful adversary defeat or pressure neutralized ticks it down by 1. Each Custodian hit or unprotected exposure ticks it up by 1. Clamp it between 0 and 10; at 0 the ward locks and the table moves to S05-07."
