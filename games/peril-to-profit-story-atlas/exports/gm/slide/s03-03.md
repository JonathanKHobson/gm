# S03-03 - The Clover Co-op

GM export - slide:S03-03

## Player text
The Clover Co-op climbs the trunk of an ancient tree, level stacked on level, crowded with music and good-natured noise. A friendly local stops you at the door: newcomers hang their shoes on the line strung across the ground floor. By the time you leave, locals promise, the shoes come back shined and filled with little trinkets. Warmth, food, and a dozen conversations wait. What do you do?

## Public objective
Rest, connect, and become someone the town trusts.

## GM goal
Use the vertical tavern as Hush's social heart: shoe custom, named locals, player-authored details, and a chance to belong.

## GM notes
- This scene is relationship, not objective extraction. Slow down and let players be people.
- Do not turn hospitality into shopping inventory; use gifts as story boons.

## GM script
Say: "This is Hush's living room. Hang your shoes if you want to honor the custom. Pick a face, pick a table, or tell me one thing this tavern has that no tavern outside Hush would have."
