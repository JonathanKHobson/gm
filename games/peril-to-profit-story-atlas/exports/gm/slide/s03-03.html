Player text

The Clover Co-op climbs the trunk of an ancient tree, level stacked on level, crowded with music and good-natured noise. A friendly local stops you at the door: newcomers hang their shoes on the line strung across the ground floor. By the time you leave, locals promise, the shoes come back shined and filled with little trinkets. Warmth, food, and a dozen conversations wait. What do you do?
