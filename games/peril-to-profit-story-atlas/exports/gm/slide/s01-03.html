S01-03 - The Creature and Her Young

GM export - slide:S01-03

Player text

From behind the cart steps a large creature: the body of a wolf, the face of an owl, broad feathered wings folded along its back. It lifts its head from the meal and studies you, unhurried, deciding whether you are a threat. Then, behind it, two smaller shapes stumble into view: clumsy, big-eyed, and keeping close to its legs.

Public objective

Deal with the creature without anyone, including the pups, getting hurt, and clear the road.

GM goal

Reveal the feeding creature by description first, then show that she is protecting pups and is not the simple monster story.

GM notes

  • Player-safe: wary mother animal, two pups, road blocked by a decision.
  • GM-only: she did not kill the driver; she is not manufactured.

GM script

Say: "She has not attacked. She is watching you and keeping herself between you and the young. If you want the name, locals call this kind of creature a strixwolf. The important part right now is not the name. It is her posture. What signal do you give her?"
