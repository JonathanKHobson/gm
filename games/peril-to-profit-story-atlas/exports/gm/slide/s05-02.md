## Player text
The Custodian lets out a shrill cry. "The Keystone has finally responded! Surround me, the ritual must begin or I'll lose the pathway! Hurry!" Her glow swells, her eyes roll back, and the carriage lifts a foot off the ground. From the woods comes a chorus of unearthly screeches. The ground rumbles. Rusted swords break the surface as dead things claw their way up. In the distance, two shapes drift toward you, screeching, wrong. Hold the line until she's done.
