## Player bullets
- Open Door: you can step away at any point and return when ready.
- Simple table signal: say pause, slow down, can we rewind, or break.
- If you missed something, ask me to repeat it.
- If I am moving too fast, ask me to slow down.
- If you are confused, ask any question at any time. There are no dumb questions here.
- If a topic is okay in the story but not for your character, say not for me.
- Discord or text can help between scenes, but I may not see it immediately while running the table.

## GM goal
Establish safety tools and participation norms.
