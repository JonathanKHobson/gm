# S05-03 - Holding the Line

GM export - slide:S05-03

## Player text
The skeletons come in a clatter of rust and bone: not clever, but endless, and all of them reaching for the glowing figure at the center. For every one you put down, the ritual's clock ticks a notch closer to done. But they keep clawing up out of the dirt, and the Custodian cannot defend herself. The line is yours to hold.

## Public objective
Defeat the rusted dead and keep the Custodian safe.

## GM goal
Run the rusted-sword skeleton wave as clear protection pressure and countdown progress.

## GM notes
- Skeletons are pressure and countdown fuel, not a slog. Keep them moving toward the Custodian.

## GM script
Say: "They are not strong. They are persistent, and they want her, not you. Stand in the way. Every one you drop moves the countdown down by one."
