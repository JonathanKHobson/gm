# ACT-THREE - Act Three: Seeking a Custodian

GM export - slide:ACT-THREE

## Player text
The road softens. The trees do not change, but the air does: warmer, kinder, like a held breath finally letting go. Ahead, lantern-light and music gather around ordinary people who feel safe. Somewhere in this village is the person you were sent to find. All you have to do now is hand off the package and collect your pay. Simple.

## Public objective
Reach the village, find the person who can receive the package, and deliver it.

## GM goal
Shift from road peril to a warm village sanctuary, then let the contrast carry unease. Establish the job plainly: reach Hush, find the person who keeps it safe, and deliver the package.

## GM notes
- High mercy from earlier acts starts Hush warmer. Slaughter or extraction starts Hush polite but cooler.
- If package_status is lost, this act can still play as arrival and social discovery, but the handoff branch is blocked until Kyle confirms the recovery path.

## GM script
Say: "The road pressure eases here. Let yourselves feel the relief. This village is safe on purpose, someone keeps it that way, and that is who you are here to find."
