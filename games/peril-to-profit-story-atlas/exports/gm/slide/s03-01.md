# S03-01 - Arrival at Hush

GM export - slide:S03-01

## Player text
A tall stone pillar rises beside the road, carved top to bottom with old protective marks and newer brass stamps. As the carriage passes it, you feel a small sensation like a bubble popping. The forest hush gives way to friendly chatter, tavern music, and smiling faces turning to wave you in. The trees are still colossal, but here the air feels safe. What do you do?

## Public objective
Enter the village, get your bearings, and find the person who keeps this place safe.

## GM goal
Run the ward-boundary crossing, the first welcome, and the first read of how prior choices affect Hush trust.

## GM notes
- Hush people are sincere. Company marks are the unease.
- Player-safe language: old protective marks, newer official stamps, the ward, the package.

## GM script
Say: "That bubble-pop is the ward. You just crossed into protected ground. If you arrived with mercy behind you, the welcome feels warmer. If the road behind you was bloodier, the welcome is polite but watchful."
