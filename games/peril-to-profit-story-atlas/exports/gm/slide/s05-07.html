Player text

An hour later, back in the treehouse, the Keystone hangs suspended over a rumbling hearth-fire. The Custodian, older now, exhaustion plain in her voice, folds herself into an over-stuffed chair. "You fought hard. I'm not surprised. Emeris doesn't send just anyone." She lets you sit with it, the warmth and the weight both. And then, because there is always one more thing: she has a question about what you will tell the people who sent you, and a map to somewhere the light is going wrong.
