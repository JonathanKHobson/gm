## Player text
As you land the blow that drops the last threat, the ritual ends. The carriage crashes back to earth and the Keystone blazes with arcane light. Then a soundless explosion erupts from the Custodian, washing outward, dissolving any enemy still standing. The screeching stops. The clawing stops. The clearing is quiet again, the open sky calm above you. It is done. The ward is renewed. You held the line, and a city you may never see will wake tomorrow still safe.
