Player bullets

  • This is an adult game, but not a grimdark or graphic game.
  • Likely content: combat, peril, ambushes, monsters, undead, corporate exploitation, fear or memory magic, and morally complicated choices.
  • Animal peril may appear. Violence against animals is optional and can be softened, avoided, or redirected.
  • If anything I describe or any action at the table makes you uncomfortable, say pause, slow down, rewind, or break.
  • The goal is to explore fantasy danger together while keeping the real table safe and communicative.

GM goal

Set a clear rating and content frame before safety tools.

GM notes

  • Script: Frame the one-shot as adult PG-13-ish. Say that combat exists, descriptions of violence will be light, animal peril can be avoided or softened, and any player can pause or redirect if something crosses a line.
  • Do not over-explain the content warning. Make it clear, quick, and normal.
  • Use the safety page for extra tools only if the table seems to need them.
  • Checklist: Adult PG-13-ish rating explained; Combat and light violence expectations explained; Optional animal peril explained; Pause/redirect permission reinforced
  • GM-only: Module contains possible animal peril, combat against conscious beings, monsters, undead, exploitation, and fear/memory magic.
