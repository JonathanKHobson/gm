# CHEAT-CONDITIONS - Conditions

GM export - slide:CHEAT-CONDITIONS

## GM goal
Apply conditions as fictional pressure, not bookkeeping.

## GM notes
- Hidden: While out of sight from all foes and they do not know where you are, rolls against you have disadvantage. Clears when: An adversary moves where they would see you, you move into line of sight, or you make an attack.
- Restrained: You cannot move, but you can still take actions from your current position. Clears when: A temporary source is cleared with a fitting action roll, a stated feature requirement, or the source ends.
- Vulnerable: You are in a difficult position in the fiction; all rolls targeting you have advantage. Clears when: A temporary source is cleared with a fitting action roll, a stated feature requirement, or the source ends.
