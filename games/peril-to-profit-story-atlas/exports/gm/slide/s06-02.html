S06-02 - The Relay Spire Hook

GM export - slide:S06-02

Player text

The Custodian leads you out of the treehouse, out of the forest itself, to look down on the green expanse from above. Twisting stone spires break through the treeline, each with a flame flickering in its tower like a beacon. "There's a relay out west," she says, "maybe a few hours. You might have felt it on the way in: its light was weaker than it should be. I think something's wrong with its Keeper, and I fear what happens if that fire goes out." She studies the horizon. "When you're ready, come back. I'll give you my map, so you can make it there safe. It's good to have heroes in the preserve again."

Public objective

Learn what is stirring west, and decide whether you leave that door open.

GM goal

The Custodian reveals another relay spire may be failing.

GM notes

  • The Relay is seen and described, not entered.
  • If epilogue_tone is pyrrhic or tragic, the hook feels like unfinished business rather than excitement.

GM script

Say: "One more thing on the horizon. Not tonight unless you want it tonight: a fire going dim, a Keeper in trouble, and a map waiting if you ever come back."
