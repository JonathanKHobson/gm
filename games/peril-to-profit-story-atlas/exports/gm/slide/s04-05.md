## Player text
The Custodian gathers what she needs with the brisk energy of someone much younger than she looks. "The Open Vale is where the ward was first forged," she says. "It is where it must be renewed." She looks at each of you in turn. "I cannot do this alone, and what comes for us when the magic rises will not be gentle. But I think you already knew the job was bigger than a crate." When you are ready, the treehouse unfolds one last time, and the road to the Open Vale opens before you.
