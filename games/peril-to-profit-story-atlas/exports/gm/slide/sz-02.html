Player text

Public objective

You do not need to justify a boundary. If something is not fun, too intense, or not for tonight, we can pause, redirect, fade to black, or move on.

Player bullets

  • Open Door: you can step away at any point and return when ready.
  • Simple table signal: say pause, slow down, can we rewind, or break.
  • If you missed something, ask me to repeat it.
  • If I am moving too fast, ask me to slow down.
  • If you are confused, ask any question at any time. There are no dumb questions here.
  • If a topic is okay in the story but not for your character, say not for me.
  • Discord or text can help between scenes, but I may not see it immediately while running the table.

GM goal

Establish safety tools and participation norms.

GM notes

  • Script: Before we play, I want to keep safety and communication simple. You can step away at any point, ask for a break, ask me to slow down or repeat something, or say pause if something is uncomfortable or confusing. I may talk too fast or interrupt sometimes; you are welcome to interrupt me, ask any question, or tell me to rewind. The story is for everyone at the table.
  • Keep private lines/veils and intake responses hidden from player display.
  • Default to lightweight in-person tools for a four-to-five-player one-shot.
  • Optional deeper tools live on the safety page for GM use only if needed.
  • If no Google Form URL is configured, show a missing-link note in GM view rather than inventing one.
  • Checklist: Lines and veils explained; Simple pause/slow down/break signal explained; Open Door explained; Break policy explained; Shared table responsibility explained; Safety intake reviewed if used
  • GM-only: Private lines and veils text area; Safety intake responses; Only sanitized summary may be exported
  • Tools: Open Door; Pause / slow down / rewind / break; Lines and Veils if needed; Not For Me, Thanks if needed; Stars and Wishes at the end if time allows
