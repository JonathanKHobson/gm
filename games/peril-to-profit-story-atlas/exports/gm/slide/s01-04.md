# S01-04 - The First Action Roll - Reading the Creature

GM export - slide:S01-04

## Player text
The creature is wary of every movement you make. Let's find out how she reacts. This is your first roll: grab your two d12s, add your Presence, and tell me the total, plus which die came up higher. If you have an Experience that fits, you can spend a Hope before you roll to add it.

## Public objective
Read the creature correctly and get everyone, including the young, out of this without bloodshed.

## GM goal
Teach Duality Dice through the creature reaction roll and resolve the standoff caused by S01-03.

## GM notes
- Use the exact outcome language here so the first roll feels transparent.
- After resolving the roll, return to the blocked road: the creature leaves, the road clears, or the encounter moves to the combat board.

## GM script
Say: "This is our first action roll. You are trying to read and influence a living creature, so this is Presence, Difficulty 10. Roll two d12s, add Presence, then tell me your total and whether Hope or Fear is higher. Before you roll, does an Experience fit strongly enough that you want to spend a Hope on it?"
