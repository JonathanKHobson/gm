Player text

The clearing is a perfect circle, the only break in the endless canopy: open sky above for the first time in days. The Custodian speaks softly as you arrive. This is where she first forged the ward pillars that keep Hush safe. She almost died doing it. Then her antennae perk in a disconcerting way. "This is good. Stop, here. Now come help me. I'm old." She begins to glow. "I'll need an hour. Enjoy the night air while you can. We'll be very busy soon."
