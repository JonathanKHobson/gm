# EPILOGUE - Epilogue: Beyond the Contract

## Player text
The screeching is gone. The clearing is quiet, the open sky calm. Somewhere west, a city you may never see is waking up safe because of what you did tonight. The Custodian is already shuffling you back toward warmth and food and a fire that does not want anything from you. For the first time since the road, there is nothing chasing you. You won. Sit with that a while.

## Public objective
Rest, reckon with what you did, and decide what you carry forward.

## GM goal
Land the plane: reward the heroes, let the table breathe, then leave one official door and one optional hidden door open.
