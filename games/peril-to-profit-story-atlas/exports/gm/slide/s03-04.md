## Player text
You have arrived during the Firstmoss Launch Festival. The town celebrates the season's first moss rising on the Sunless Farms crops, the promise of fresh fruit and full tables ahead. There is a good-natured arm-wrestling competition, a stone-painting class, and a small market of homemade trinkets. The whole village feels lighter for a moment. What do you do?

## Public objective
Join the celebration if you want, earn goodwill, and gather what helps for the road ahead.

## GM goal
Offer the optional reward arc: a joyful festival, low-stakes contests, a concrete boon, and one quiet seed about the farms.

## GM notes
- The festival is the act's exhale before the Act 4 turn.
- Boon examples: charm, favor token, useful market find, or goodwill that opens a door later.

## GM script
