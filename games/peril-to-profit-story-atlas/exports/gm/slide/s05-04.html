S05-04 - The Soul-Audit Wraiths

GM export - slide:S05-04

Player text

The drifting things reach you, and they do not swing for your body. One presses a cold hand to a face and reaches inside, dredging up something buried. The other simply passes through a body like a door, and for a horrible moment a soul stands outside itself, unable to move, unable to act, while the fight roars on without them. These are worse than the dead. These ask questions you do not want to answer.

Public objective

Survive the wraiths, protect each other, and keep the ritual on track.

GM goal

Escalate into wraith pressure where memory, vulnerability, and rescue become the emotional core.

GM notes

  • Use Pass-Through sparingly. Rescue and grounding should return players to action quickly.

GM script

Say: "These do not want your blood. They want your worst memory and your soul out of your body. If someone gets knocked loose, protecting and calling them back is the scene."
