# S01-02 - The Overturned Vendor Cart

GM export - slide:S01-02

## Player text
Your steeds haul the carriage around a tight bend and stop hard. The path ahead is blocked: a vendor cart lies overturned, its fruit, vegetables, and spilled preserves scattered across the trail. Something has been feeding here. The air is wrong: sweet rot and something underneath it, coppery. From the far side of the cart, you hear wet, unhurried movement.

## Public objective
Figure out what happened here and clear the road.

## GM goal
Block the road with a wrecked vendor cart, let the table read the scene, and plant that the obvious animal story does not add up.

## GM notes
- Use the wreck as an investigation scene first and a creature reveal second.
- Player-safe language: body, feeding, blade wound. GM-only truth: staged murder and later Bramble connection.

## GM script
Say: "The carriage stops hard. Ahead of you is a wreck, a body, and something feeding just out of sight. You can rush toward the sound, but you have time to look first. What do you check?" If someone checks the body, say: "The feeding is messy, but the killing wound is clean. This was not done by claws." If they need the roll, call Knowledge or Instinct, Difficulty 12.
