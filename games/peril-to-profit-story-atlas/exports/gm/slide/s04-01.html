S04-01 - Through the Farms

GM export - slide:S04-01

Player text

Past the last homes of the village, the crops change: fruits and vegetables wear a thin layer of glowing blue moss that pulses softly with warmth as you pass. Then the cultivated land ends, and the preserve returns. The great trees here have faces, hundreds of them, carved into every side. Ahead, something hangs in the canopy: a cabin, swaying gently, suspended over a counterweight stone the size of a cabin itself.

Public objective

Reach the Custodian's home.

GM goal

Cross from Hush farmland into the carved-face preserve edge and pay off the route the party earned.

GM notes

  • This is the Act 3 to Act 4 consequence handoff: route quality changes approach pressure.
  • The alternate hazard is minor and non-combat. Save lethal danger for Act 5.

GM script

Say: "This is where the route you earned pays off. If Hush trusted you, the way is calm. If the route is dangerous or unknown, the preserve asks for one last bit of attention before the treehouse."
