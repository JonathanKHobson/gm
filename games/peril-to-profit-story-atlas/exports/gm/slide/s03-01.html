Player text

A tall stone pillar rises beside the road, carved top to bottom with old protective marks and newer brass stamps. As the carriage passes it, you feel a small sensation like a bubble popping. The forest hush gives way to friendly chatter, tavern music, and smiling faces turning to wave you in. The trees are still colossal, but here the air feels safe. What do you do?
