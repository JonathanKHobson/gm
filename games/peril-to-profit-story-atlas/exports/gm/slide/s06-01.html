Player text

An hour later, back in the treehouse, the Keystone hangs suspended above a rumbling hearth-fire, glowing soft and settled. The Custodian, older now, the night's work plain in her voice, folds into an over-stuffed chair. "You fought hard. I'm not surprised. Emeris doesn't send just anyone." There is hot food, a warm bath waiting, and for the first time in days, nowhere you need to be. She looks at each of you like she is memorizing you. "Before you go," she says, "let's talk about what happened out there, and what you'll do with it."
