At the edge of the warded road, a woman waits beside a loaded carriage. She looks like your contact for this job, but she has not introduced herself yet.

Public objective

Meet the contact, take custody of the sealed package, ask what you need to ask, and decide to roll out.

GM goal

The party meets their contact by description, receives the sealed package, asks questions, and agrees to roll out.

GM notes

  • Do not play Emeris Crown Holdings as the villain yet. They are the employer and the system. Let suspicion emerge slowly.
  • Keep player-facing information actionable. Reveal systemic truth through objects, reactions, and consequences before naming it.
  • Manufactured-threat lens: seed the contradiction, do not explain it. The surface goal stays simple and heroic.
  • [ADD/GUARANTEED] Grail-branded bounty board: "STRIXWOLF: APEX FOREST HAZARD. Sightings up 300%. Bounty payable in Marks."
  • [ADD/GUARANTEED] Couriers or a clerk trade fearful route gossip: the Preserve "takes one a week now."
  • [ADD/EARNED] A player asking what they know receives the corporate line: strixwolves are vicious pack predators. (Knowledge Easy 10)

GM script

At the edge of the warded road, a woman waits beside a loaded carriage. She has a book, a folded route-map, and the posture of someone trying not to look nervous. She looks across the group and says, 'You're the crew? Good. We're losing daylight.' What do you do?
