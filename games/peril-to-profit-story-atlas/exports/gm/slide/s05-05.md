## Player text
It all comes at once: the dead clawing up faster, the wraiths pressing in, the Custodian's glow guttering like a candle in wind. The clock is close, but so is disaster. Every choice now costs something: guard her and leave a friend exposed, or save the friend and let her take the hit that sets you all back. There is no clean way through. There is only what you are willing to give.

## Public objective
Survive the peak and drive the countdown home.

## GM goal
Run the peak HOLD/BREAK choice and let resource spends, protection, and sacrifice decide the outcome track.

## GM notes
- No PC is required to land the final blow. Let any meaningful action create the final tick if fiction supports it.

## GM script
Say: "All-in time. Spend Hope, boons, big abilities, tag-team moments, and last good ideas. You cannot cover it all, so what you choose to save becomes the ending."
