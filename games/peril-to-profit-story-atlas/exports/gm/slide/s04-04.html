S04-04 - The Keystone Reveal

GM export - slide:S04-04

Player text

The Custodian unfolds her home like a blossoming flower and draws the crate inside, sealing it open again as a workshop. She breaks the seal. Inside lies a massive stone carved with a lion's face. She goes still, then nods, slow and grave. "Of course Emeris would keep this delivery quiet," she says. "This is the Keystone Asset: the heart-stone for Hush's failing ward. If the route knew what you carried, every hungry thing in the preserve would follow the scent of it." The light in her home dims as she speaks. "We must take it to the Open Vale and revitalize the ward before Hush has to trust paper promises instead of protection. That much magic will draw dangerous things from the deep preserve. I will need your help."
