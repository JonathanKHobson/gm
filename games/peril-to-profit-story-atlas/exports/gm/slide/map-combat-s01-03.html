GM notes

  • Winning does not mean killing her. Winning means road cleared and no one made the world worse.

GM script

Say: "This is not a monster hunt. This is a defensive standoff. Every turn, you can escalate by threatening her or the young, or de-escalate by creating space, lowering weapons, opening the pups' escape route, or calming her."
