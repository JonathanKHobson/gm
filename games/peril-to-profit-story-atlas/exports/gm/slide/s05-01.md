# S05-01 - The Open Vale Site

GM export - slide:S05-01

## Player text
The clearing is a perfect circle, the only break in the endless canopy: open sky above for the first time in days. The Custodian speaks softly as you arrive. This is where she first forged the ward pillars that keep Hush safe. She almost died doing it. Then her antennae perk in a disconcerting way. "This is good. Stop, here. Now come help me. I'm old." She begins to glow. "I'll need an hour. Enjoy the night air while you can. We'll be very busy soon."

## Public objective
Reach the ritual site, learn what is coming, and prepare.

## GM goal
Arrive at the old ritual clearing, run the last Short Rest, and turn preparation into finale resources.

## GM notes
- This is the pressure valve before the finale. Give every player one prep spotlight before moving on.

## GM script
Say: "This is your last calm moment. Short Rest now: Tend to Wounds, Clear Stress, Repair Armor, or Prepare for Hope. Pick two. If you Prepare together, you each get two Hope. I take 1d4 Fear during the rest."
