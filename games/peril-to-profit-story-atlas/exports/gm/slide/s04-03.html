S04-03 - The Whitefire Custodian

GM export - slide:S04-03

Player text

The home lowers, and from it steps a being seven feet tall: part humanoid, part firefly, moving in slow drifts broken by sudden jerky motions. Her face is hard to read, but her voice carries every feeling plainly. She eyes your group with open mischief. "Are you the group Emeris sent from the capital? Oh my. You are rather late, are you not? Come in, come in." Inside, the treehouse opens far larger than it should, packed with potion bottles, spellbooks, runes, plants, and small creatures. None of it is messy. You get the sense that if you moved a single item an inch, she would know.

Public objective

Win the Custodian's confidence and hand over the package.

GM goal

Deliver the Custodian as the RP heart of Act 4 and let the party earn her trust before the reveal.
