Player text

Up close, cabin undersells it. A home hangs from a braid of rope as thick as a giant's forearm, tied to a massive branch and counterweighted by a boulder the size of the cabin itself. The whole thing sways like overripe fruit ready to drop. The boulder is carved with symbols; the windows flash a soft yellow-green light. There is no obvious door at ground level. What do you do?

Public objective

Get the Custodian's attention or reach her home.

GM goal

Run the hanging-home puzzle as a gentle test of patience, courtesy, and cleverness.

GM notes

  • The goal is not to stump the table. Let the puzzle reveal what kind of guests they are.
  • Destructive force lowers trust; gentle strength or patient honesty raises it.

GM script

Say: "This is not a lock to force. It is a person to convince. Call up, climb, study the counterweight, or wait and announce yourselves honestly."
