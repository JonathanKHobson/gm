Strixwolf Pup One

Creature / Animal

Player-safe note: A wet-feathered strixwolf pup with oversized ears, bright frightened eyes, and paws too large for the body it has not grown into yet.

Observed Form

Young owl-wolf body, mottled fur, downy dark wing-feathers along the back, upright ears, and cautious forward posture.

Behavior

Pure startled instinct in a body it has not grown into yet. Bright frightened eyes, ears that swivel at every snapped twig, paws a size too big. It wants its mother and cannot understand why the road brought strangers instead.

Warning Signs

Loud voices, hands that reach too fast, and the open ground between itself and its mother.

You Could -

  • Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
  • Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
