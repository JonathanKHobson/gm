# Strixwolf Mother

Field Guide Page

## Hook
a huge owl-faced wolf mother guarding her pups near the wreck

## Observed Form
A huge owl-wolf mother crouches around her pups, barred feathers and fur bristling, amber eyes fixed on any hand that comes too close.

## Behavior
Low, protective, ready to retreat if retreat becomes safer than warning.

## Warning Signs
A sudden rush toward the pups, fire, and cornered noise.

## You Could
- Give it distance before deciding it is hostile.
- Read posture, tracks, and what it is protecting.
