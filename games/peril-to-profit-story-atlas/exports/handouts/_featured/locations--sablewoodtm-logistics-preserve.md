# Sablewood™ Logistics Preserve

Goldspire Travel Advisory

## Hook
An ancient forest logistics preserve where colossal trees, hybrid fauna, underroot communities, route tolls, and corporate liability language all compete to define what safety means.

## Arrival Read
Sablewood is an ancient forest forced to wear a logistics badge: colossal trunks, liability signs, toll language, and living routes that do not care about forms.

## Known For
The trees go up until the sky closes over, and the light that reaches the road is green and always moving. A vest-badge nailed to a trunk reads Sablewood Logistics Preserve, and a hundred yards on the same kind of trunk has swallowed an older sign whole...
