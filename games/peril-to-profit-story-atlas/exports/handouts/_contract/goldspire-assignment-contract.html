How Rolls Work

When your character does something uncertain and consequences matter, the GM may call for an action roll. Roll a Hope d12 and a Fear d12, add the fitting trait, and compare the total to the difficulty. If Hope is higher, you gain Hope. If Fear is higher, the GM gains Fear. Matching dice are a critical success. The GM will teach this at the table, one roll at a time.

Player Wellness

This policy is for players, not characters. At any time, you may ask for a Wellness Pause, a rewind, a break, or a topic moved off-screen. No explanation is required. The table adjusts at once, with no penalty and no debate.
