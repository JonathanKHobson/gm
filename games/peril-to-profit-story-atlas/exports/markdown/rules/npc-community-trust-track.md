# NPC / Community Trust Track

RAW export - rule:npc-community-trust-track

## What it is
A module-facing tracker for rapport with a person or settlement.

## How it works
Use when repeated choices change how warm, candid, generous, or guarded an NPC/community becomes.

## Step by step
- Use when repeated choices change how warm, candid, generous, or guarded an NPC/community becomes.
- High trust makes help warmer and clearer. Low trust still lets play continue, but with distance, fewer gifts, or harder social positioning.
- Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.
