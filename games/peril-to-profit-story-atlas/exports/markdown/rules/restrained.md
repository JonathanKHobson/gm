# Restrained

RAW export - rule:restrained

## What it is
A target cannot move freely, though it may still act from position.

## How it works
Use when physically trapped, grabbed, bound, or pinned.

## Step by step
- Use when physically trapped, grabbed, bound, or pinned.
- If the fiction says they are physically trapped, yes.
- Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.
