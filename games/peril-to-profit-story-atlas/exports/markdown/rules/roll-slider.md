# Roll Slider

RAW export - rule:roll-slider

## What it is
Kyle's optional degree-of-success overlay: the total says how far above or below Difficulty the result lands while Hope/Fear sets the spin.

## How it works
Use when a clue, negotiation, or set piece benefits from richer tiers than pass/fail.

## Step by step
- Use when a clue, negotiation, or set piece benefits from richer tiers than pass/fail.
- It tells you how much they get: botch, miss, hit, clean, soar, or crit, while Hope/Fear still colors the result.
- Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.
