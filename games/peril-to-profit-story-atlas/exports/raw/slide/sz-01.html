Player bullets

  • Concept: a corporate-fantasy delivery job that becomes morally complicated.
  • Aim: learn Daggerheart, build the party, protect a community, and discover what the sealed delivery means.
  • Tone: mostly sincere fantasy adventure, with corporate satire and dark whimsy around the edges.
  • Subject matter: fantasy danger, animal peril, ambush, death, corporate exploitation, undead, and memory or fear magic.
