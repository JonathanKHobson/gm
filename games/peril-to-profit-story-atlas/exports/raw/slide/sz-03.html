SZ-03 - Daggerheart Rules Snapshot

RAW export - slide:SZ-03

Player text

Daggerheart is narrative fantasy. The GM describes the world, players describe what their characters do, and we roll when the outcome is risky, uncertain, or dramatic.

Public objective

Daggerheart is narrative fantasy. The GM describes the world, players describe what their characters do, and we roll when the outcome is risky, uncertain, or dramatic.

Player bullets

  • Describe what your character tries to do.
  • If it is risky or uncertain, roll Hope and Fear d12s.
  • Add the trait that fits your approach.
  • Tell the GM the total and whether Hope or Fear was higher.
  • We will learn the rest as it matters.
