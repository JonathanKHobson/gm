Five Roll Outcomes

RAW export - rule:five-outcomes

What it is

Every action roll resolves into one of five table results: critical success, success with Hope, success with Fear, failure with Hope, or failure with Fear.

How it works

Use after every Action Roll or Reaction Roll where the Hope/Fear spin matters.

Step by step

  • Check doubles first for Critical Success.
  • If not a critical, compare total to Difficulty.
  • At or above Difficulty succeeds; below Difficulty fails.
  • Hope spin gives Hope and a cleaner/kinder edge; Fear spin gives the GM Fear and a cost or complication.
