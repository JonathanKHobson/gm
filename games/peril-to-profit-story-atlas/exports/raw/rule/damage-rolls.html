Damage Rolls

RAW export - rule:damage-rolls

What it is

After a successful attack, roll the listed weapon, spell, or adversary damage dice and compare the total to thresholds when needed.

How it works

Use after a hit or when a feature calls for damage.

Step by step

  • Use after a hit or when a feature calls for damage.
  • After the attack succeeds. Roll the listed damage, then compare it to the target's thresholds or stat block instructions.
  • Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.
