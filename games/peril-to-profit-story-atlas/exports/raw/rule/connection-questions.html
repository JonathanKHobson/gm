What it is

Daggerheart-style prompts that let players define safe, playable relationships before the first scene.

How it works

Use during Session Zero, character introductions, and optional party-bond refresh beats.

Step by step

  • Use during Session Zero, character introductions, and optional party-bond refresh beats.
  • Offer one relationship prompt, then let the other player answer in their own words and keep only the part both players like.
  • Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.

  • Summary: Daggerheart-style prompts that let players define safe, playable relationships before the first scene.
  • When: Use during Session Zero, character introductions, and optional party-bond refresh beats.
  • Not When: Do not force a player to accept another player's premise; the answer can accept, alter, soften, or pass.
  • Question: What does a connection answer look like?
  • Answer: Offer one relationship prompt, then let the other player answer in their own words and keep only the part both players like.
  • Example: Varian asks, 'You saw me freeze once. What did you do?' The other player answers with a small memory the table can use later.
