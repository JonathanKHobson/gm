## Action Roll
- Roll when a PC does something uncertain and consequences matter.
- Player states the action in the fiction.
- GM names the trait that best matches the method and the Difficulty.
- Player rolls Hope d12 + Fear d12 and adds trait, Experience, or advantage/disadvantage.
- Compare total to Difficulty, then resolve the Hope/Fear or critical result.

## Duality Dice
- Roll a Hope d12 and Fear d12 together, add modifiers, compare to Difficulty.
- Use for Action Rolls and most player-facing uncertain rolls.
- One sets the total with the other; the higher die colors the result with Hope or Fear.
- Return to the fiction with the concrete result, cost, or benefit.

## Hope
- Player resource gained from Hope results and spent to help or use features.
