Stargate Card Universe

Custom cards with faction identity you can feel.

A fan/personal Magic-style design project translating Stargate factions into playable mechanics, rules text, flavor, and balance knobs.

4 core factions Real Cockatrice exports Rules + flavor + tuning

What This Game Shows

The Stargate card project shows system design through a different doorway: faction identity, balance budgets, readable rules text, and flavor that teaches the player what the deck wants to do.

The working card files cover Earth, Goa'uld, Wraith, and Replicators, with mana costs, rules text, flavor text, budget totals, fairness notes, and tuning notes. The preview uses real Cockatrice card exports, not generated sci-fi stand-ins.

  • Turns source-fiction themes into mechanical identities.
  • Uses card text, costs, and faction constraints to make strategy readable.
  • Shows system-building beyond roleplay scenes: cards, balance, and playtest thinking.

What to notice

Faction flavor, costs, rules text, and table-readable constraints show up in one artifact.
