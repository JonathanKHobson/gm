# Copy Rewrites — Voice Guardrails (read first)

These rewrites are surgical. The brand voice is a **strength and an asset** — it is canon to the Peril to Profit world (the campaign's organizations are literally `Kazrak Industries™`, `Soulspire Solutions™`, `Hexmart™`, with "Management" and "Compliance" as institutions). Per the P2P-GM-MCP canon, corporate-fantasy satire is the house style, not decoration.

## The one rule the focus group agreed on
> **Joke in the prose. Clarity in the label.**
Keep every gag in sentences and microcopy. Let **navigation, eyebrows, and headings** state the literal function so a nervous newcomer never has to decode whether a label is a joke or real information.

## Does the copy "feel AI"? — No.
The writing reads as authored: specific, tonally disciplined, with jokes too particular to be generic LLM output ("Ignore any humming not relevant to your pay tier," "the sheet looks like a tax document with a sword," "That clause survived legal review"). **The only place copy edges toward templated is the reassurance boilerplate repeated 6+ times** (fixed in [02](02-section-labels-and-faq.md)).

## Keep (do not touch)
- The H1, the corporate-notice framing, "Management/Compliance/corporate-approved," the courier conceit.
- Microlines like "Adventure mandatory; survival encouraged," "Management thanks you for your interest in surviving adventurously."
- The FAQ answers' warmth and the "tax document with a sword" line.

## Change only where it (a) blocks a task, (b) hides a fact a nervous reader needs, or (c) repeats to the point of boilerplate.
