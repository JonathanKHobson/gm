# Copy Rewrites — Section Labels, Eyebrows & Reassurance Density

## A. Satirical eyebrows that hide their function (Low–Med) → [BUG-08](../bugs/BUG-08-satire-labels-scannability/report.md)

Rule: **joke in the prose, clarity in the label.** Keep the satire in the body copy; make the eyebrow/heading say what the section *is* so a nervous newcomer can scan. Two patterns work: (1) plain eyebrow + satirical heading, or (2) "Plain — satirical aside" eyebrow.

| Section (live eyebrow) | Issue | Proposed (keeps voice, adds clarity) |
|---|---|---|
| `Provisional hire info` (logistics: when/where/price/bring) | Reads as a joke; a newcomer isn't sure it's the practical details | **`Event details`** (eyebrow) + keep the satirical heading "Bring yourself and enough time to stay for the session." |
| `Things Management is willing to clarify` (the FAQ) | Charming but doesn't say "FAQ" | **`FAQ · Things Management is willing to clarify`** — literal word first, joke second |
| `What you will do at the table` → cards "Training Memo / Safety Update / Crew Protocol" | The card titles are pure satire; a newcomer skimming for "what happens / safety / how we treat each other" may miss it | Add a plain 2–3 word descriptor under each satirical title: **Training Memo** *(how a session flows)* · **Safety Update** *(tone & content)* · **Crew Protocol** *(table etiquette)* |
| `Route preview` / "The road has passed at least one inspection." | Fine — eyebrow is literal, heading is the joke. **Keep.** | — |
| `New adventurer onboarding` | Fine. Keep. | — |

*Net effect: zero jokes removed; every section becomes scannable for the Total Newcomer and Reluctant Plus-One.*

## B. Reassurance repetition (Low) → master report F9

The "no experience / heroes provided / rules taught / no homework" promise appears in: hero lede, gold corporate-notice strip, the "Coming from Discord" path card, the beginners band chips, and three FAQ answers. That's 6+ repetitions.

**Keep the strongest placements:**
1. Hero lede (decision moment)
2. Gold corporate-notice strip (skimmer's eye-catch)
3. FAQ (for those who came looking)

**Trim/vary the rest** so it stops reading as boilerplate:
- Beginners band: change the four chips from another restatement of "no experience" to **distinct** reassurances — e.g. `Premade heroes` · `Rules as you go` · `Ask anything` · `No voices required`. (These are *specific*, not a fourth "don't worry.")
- "Coming from Discord" path card: keep the logistics ($25 / 4-hour / Mox), drop the repeated "intro-friendly" since the strip just said it.

## C. FAQ — small wins (Low)

The FAQ is strong. Two micro-tweaks:
- Add one Q the system-tourists want: **"I've played D&D but not Daggerheart — is that fine?"** → "Yes. No Daggerheart experience needed; if you know another RPG you'll pick it up in minutes, and premades mean zero setup."
- Add the question every Connection Seeker asks: **"Will there be more sessions after this?"** → a one-line honest answer (even "maybe — come find out" with a contact nudge) converts community-seekers. (Ties to [BUG-05](../bugs/BUG-05-no-social-proof/report.md).)
