# Usability Test — Persona Walkthroughs (Synthetic)

Moderator-led think-aloud transcripts. Synthetic/host-interpreted on live evidence. Task key: ✅ success · ⚠️ friction · ❌ blocked.

## P1 — The Total Newcomer

**Think-aloud:**
> "Okay… 'Peril to Profit: The Goldspire Messengers.' The little line says 'Beginner-friendly Daggerheart one-shot,' good, that's me. The big line — 'A simple delivery. A suspicious crate. One night of cinematic fantasy chaos' — that's fun, not scary. And right under it: 'No tabletop experience needed. Heroes provided, rules taught as you play, and acting voices are entirely optional.' That's *exactly* the thing I was afraid of — being made to act. Relief."
> "Price, date, time are right there in little boxes. Four hours… that's a real commitment but okay. 'Five seats only' — oh, so I should decide soon."
> "I scroll and it keeps reassuring me — 'Never played before? Excellent.' 'Questions welcome.' 'No acting required.' Honestly after the third time I get it; I almost wonder if it's protesting too much."
> "FAQ is great — 'Do I have to act or do a voice? No.' Perfect. I'm in. I click **Claim a Seat**… and it says the listing is 'being finalized.' Oh. So I can't actually sign up. It says ask Kyle a question. I don't have a question, I have a yes. …I'll come back later." *(probably won't)*

**Tasks:** T1 ✅ · T2 ✅ · T3 ✅ (best-served persona for fear relief) · T4 ✅ · T5 ❌ **blocked at coming-soon** · T6 ⚠️ (scrolls fine but header nav gone on phone)
**Exit:** Satisfaction 4/5 for the page, **conversion 0** because of T5. "Loved the tone, felt safe, then couldn't say yes."
**Make-or-break:** Reassurance converts them; the dead-end loses them. They will not chase a `mailto`.

**Tasks:** T1 ⚠️ (worries it's too beginner) · T2 ✅ · T3 ✅ · T4 ✅ (resources page is a plus) · T5 ❌ · T6 ⚠️

**Make-or-break:** The explicit "no Daggerheart experience needed" line, weakened on live (see BUG-09), is their reassurance — restore it above the fold.

## P5 — The Connection Seeker

**Think-aloud:**
> "New to town, want a hobby with actual people. The warmth and humor tell me the table's vibe — that matters more to me than the rules. 'Share spotlight,' 'questions welcome,' 'beginner-friendly' — sounds like my people."
> "But… are these *actually* my people? I'd love a quote from a past player, or a face, or a hint that this becomes a regular thing. The page is all promise, no proof. Is this a one-time thing or could I find a group?"
> "I'd take the audition. Register… coming soon."

**Tasks:** T1 ✅ · T2 ✅ · T3 ✅ · T4 ✅ · T5 ❌ · T6 ⚠️
**Exit:** 3.5/5. "Show me it's real people, and tell me if there's a next session."
**Make-or-break:** Needs **social proof** (BUG-05) and a "more sessions may follow" hint. Then the dead-end still blocks them.
