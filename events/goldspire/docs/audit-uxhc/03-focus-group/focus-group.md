# Focus Group — Synthetic Panel Discussion

**Format:** Moderator + the seven personas, post-task group discussion. No human in the loop; synthetic and host-interpreted on live evidence. Goal: surface where personas *agree*, where they *conflict*, and what they'd prioritize.

---

## Round 1 — "First reaction, one sentence."

- **P7 (Severance Fan):** "Funniest event listing I've seen — the corporate-fantasy bit is the whole reason I clicked."
- **P1 (Total Newcomer):** "It made me feel safe before it made me laugh, which is the right order for me."
- **P3 (Critical Role Curious):** "It's clearly built for people exactly like me — watched everything, played nothing."
- **P4 (Mox Regular):** "I trusted it the second I saw 'Mox Boarding House Chandler.'"
- **P2 (Daggerheart Tourist):** "Good, but it talks to beginners so much I briefly wondered if it was beneath me."
- **P5 (Connection Seeker):** "Lovely vibe. I just couldn't tell if it's real people or a nice poster."
- **P6 (Reluctant Plus-One):** "If my friend showed me this, the 'you don't have to perform' lines would get my yes."

## Round 2 — "Where did it break for you?"

> **Moderator:** Show of hands — who tried to sign up?
> *(All but P6 raise hands.)*
> **Moderator:** Who succeeded?
> *(Silence.)*

- **P3:** "Coming soon. I had my card out. That's the most painful place to stop me."
- **P4:** "I'll just ask staff — but only because I'm there anyway. A stranger wouldn't."
- **P1:** "It told me to 'ask Kyle a question.' I didn't have a question. I had a yes. There was nowhere to put it."
- **P5:** "Same. And there's no way to say 'tell me when it opens.' So I'll forget."
- **P7:** "At least the dead-end was funny. 'That clause survived legal review.' Still a dead-end though."

> **Moderator:** On your phones — could you reach the FAQ from the top?
- **P1:** "No links up top, just the logo and the button. I had to scroll and hunt."
- **P2:** "On the Daggerheart page the phone nav worked fine, weirdly. Just not the event page."

## Round 3 — "What would make you trust it more?"

- **P5:** "A real quote from someone who went. A face. Proof it's humans."
- **P6:** "Knowing there's a forgiving character I can hide behind — I saw 'Easiest start,' that helped."
- **P2:** "Say 'no Daggerheart experience needed' right at the top, not buried."
- **P4:** "Honestly just a working button. I'm already sold."
- **P3:** "Tell me it really ends in one night. It does say that — keep saying it."

## Round 4 — Conflict: "Is the humor too much?"

This is the one place personas *disagree*, and it matters for the copy decisions.

- **P7:** "Don't you dare tone it down. The satire is the product."
- **P1:** "I love the jokes, but when a section is called 'Provisional hire info' I have to stop and decode that it means 'the practical details.' Twice I wasn't sure if a label was a joke or real info."
- **P2:** "Agreed with P1 — keep the jokes in the sentences, but let the headings tell me plainly what's in the box."
- **P7:** "Fine — joke in the body, signpost in the heading. I can live with that."
- **Moderator (synthesis):** Consensus lands on **'voice in the prose, clarity in the labels.'** Nobody wants fewer jokes; the nervous personas want the *navigation/labels* to stay literal so the jokes are a reward, not a toll.

## Round 5 — "Rank what to fix first." (group vote)

| Priority | Fix | Votes | Champions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Make "Claim a Seat" actually work (or capture emails now) | 7/7 | everyone |
| 2 | Fix mobile header navigation | 5/7 | P1, P2, P3, P5, P6 |
| 3 | Add player testimonials / proof it's real people | 4/7 | P5, P6, P1, P2 |
| 4 | Put "no Daggerheart experience needed" above the fold | 3/7 | P2, P3, P5 |
| 5 | Keep jokes, clarify section labels | 3/7 | P1, P2, P6 |
| — | Add a closing CTA + footer on the Daggerheart page | 2/7 | P2, P3 |

## Consensus statement

> "This page already did the hard part — it made strangers want to come. It's losing them on the easy part: letting them say yes. Fix the signup, fix the phone menu, prove it's real people, and keep every single joke."
