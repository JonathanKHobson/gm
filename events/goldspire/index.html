Who this is for

New players are expected here.

This table is built for people who are new to tabletop roleplaying, new to Daggerheart, or just unsure whether they will know what to do. You do not need to perform a voice, know the right move, or read rules before you arrive.

Kyle will teach the rules in plain language, walk through the character sheets, and help you make choices at the table. You can speak in character, summarize what your character does, or ask for help finding the words.

One-shot premise

One delivery. One strange forest road. One contract that probably left out the important parts.

You are part of a courier crew hired for a delivery run through an old forest now managed with polished signs, cheerful slogans, and the kind of corporate confidence that usually means someone skipped a site inspection.

The job looks simple: protect the package, follow the road, reach the destination, get paid. The forest, unfortunately, appears to have its own onboarding process.

Everything else comes through play.

What is Daggerheart?

Daggerheart is a fantasy tabletop roleplaying game from Darrington Press. It uses Hope and Fear dice to keep scenes moving with heroic momentum and interesting complications.

You do not need to learn it before the event. Kyle will teach what matters as it appears in play.

Is this okay if I have never played?

Yes. This event expects first-time players. You will get a premade character, a guided start, and plain-language rules help.

Will I be okay with one-shot pace?

Yes. A one-shot is designed to start, build, and end in one session. Kyle keeps the table moving, invites quieter players in, and helps decisions stay clear.

Do I need experience or prep?

No. Bring yourself. Dice are optional if you have them. Character sheets and rules support are provided at the table.

Do I have to act or do a voice?

No. You can speak in first person, third person, or plain description. "I ask what happened" and "Marlowe tries to calm the creature" are both valid play.

What kind of content appears?

Expect fantasy combat, magical danger, strange creatures, spooky moments, implied danger, and corporate satire. The game is not intended to be graphic, cruel, or player-vs-player.
