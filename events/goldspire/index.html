What you'll play

One simple delivery job. One strange forest road. One contract with omissions.

You are part of a courier crew hired to carry protected magical cargo through the Goldspire Territories to a small forest community that needs help.

The route has polished waymarkers, cheerful compliance language, and a little too much confidence for a forest this old. You know the job matters. You know the cargo stays protected. You know payment follows delivery.

Everything else comes through play.

The table pitch

A warm, cinematic fantasy adventure with action, comedy, teamwork, and light corporate-fantasy satire.

Beginner promise

New players are expected here.

You do not need to know Daggerheart. You do not need tabletop experience. You do not need to make a character before the game.

Kyle will teach the rules at the table, walk through the character sheets, and help you make choices in plain language. You can speak in character, summarize what your character does, or ask for help finding the words.

Premade characters provided. Just show up.

Tone and comfort

Warm, weird, cinematic fantasy with light corporate satire.

Expect strange forest danger, roleplay, clues, action, and heroic choices. You do not need to perform a voice, know every rule, or guess the correct answer.

Good play means engaging with the situation, sharing spotlight, and helping the table have a good time.

Content note: fantasy combat, magical danger, strange creatures, spooky moments, implied death, and corporate satire. The game is not intended to be grim, graphic, cruel, or player-vs-player.

Table feel

Action, comedy, teamwork, and enough corporate paperwork jokes to make the danger feel suspiciously well-branded.

FAQ

The fast answers.

Do I need Daggerheart experience?

No. The rules will be taught at the table.

What is a tabletop roleplaying game (TTRPG)?

It is a shared story game: part game night, part interactive theater, part heroic group problem-solving. You describe what your character tries, dice help with uncertain moments, and the table discovers what happens next together.

Do I need TTRPG experience?

No. This is built for first-time players and curious beginners.

Do I need to make a character?

No. Premade characters are provided.

Can experienced players join?

Yes. The table is beginner-friendly, not beginner-only.

Is this a campaign?

No. This is a one-shot designed to finish in one session.

What if I am nervous?

That is normal. The session starts with guided onboarding, simple character choices, and room to ask questions.

Do I have to act or do a voice?

No. You can speak in first person, third person, or plain description.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes, if they also claim a seat. There are 5 seats max.

What if I sign up and cannot attend?

Please cancel or message as soon as possible so the seat can go to someone else.
