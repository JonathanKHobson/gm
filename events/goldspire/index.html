New adventurer onboarding

Never played a tabletop roleplaying game before? Excellent.

This table is built for people who are new to tabletop roleplaying, new to Daggerheart, or coming from another game system. You do not need to know rules, own dice, build a character, or perform a voice before you arrive.

Kyle will teach the rules in plain language, walk through the character sheets, and help you make choices at the table. You can speak in character, describe what your hero does, ask for help, or take a moment to think. Questions are normal.

Your assignment

Take the job. Protect the magical cargo. Watch the road go wildly off-script.

You are part of a courier crew hired for a simple delivery through the Goldspire Territories: carry a magical ward-stone along an old forest route with polished signs, cheerful slogans, and the kind of corporate confidence that usually means someone skipped a site inspection.

The job looks simple: protect the cargo, follow the road, reach the destination, get paid. Then the road gets strange, the paperwork gets less helpful, and heroism becomes suspiciously mandatory.

Everything else comes through play.

FAQ

Questions you can open only if you need them.

The first fold gives you enough to decide. These answers are here for reassurance, not homework.

What is Daggerheart?

Daggerheart is a fantasy tabletop roleplaying game from Darrington Press. If you know D&D or other RPGs, think heroic fantasy with a different dice engine and more momentum around Hope and Fear.

You do not need to learn it before the event. Kyle will teach what matters as it appears in play.

Is this okay if I have never played?

Yes. This event expects and welcomes first-time players. You will get a premade character, a guided start, and plain-language rules help.

Will I be okay with one-shot pace?

Yes. A one-shot is designed to start, build, and end in one session. Kyle keeps the table moving, invites quieter players in, and helps decisions stay clear.

Do I need experience or prep?

No. Bring yourself. Dice are optional if you have them. Character sheets and rules support are provided at the table. No Daggerheart experience or prior heroic employment required.

Will there be another one-shot like this?

If the table chemistry and demand are good, Kyle may open additional Goldspire one-shot seats.

Want to be on the table list early? Use the contact form at the bottom of the GM page: Book me.

Do I have to act or do a voice?

No. You can speak in first person, third person, or plain description. "I ask what happened" and "Marlowe tries to calm the creature" are both valid play.

What kind of content appears?

Expect fantasy combat, magical danger, strange creatures, spooky moments, implied danger, and corporate satire. The game is not intended to be graphic, cruel, or player-vs-player.
