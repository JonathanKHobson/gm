Ready-to-play heroes

Choose the job that sounds fun.

Seven premade heroes are ready at the table. Compare their specialties, pick two favorites, and let Kyle teach the few features that matter first.

You do not need to build, print, or study a character before the session.

A simple way to choose

Role first. Details later.

  1. Pick the team function that sounds satisfying.
  2. Notice the complexity label and one play promise.
  3. Choose a first and second favorite.

Kyle confirms availability before the mission. No role is mandatory, and you can ask to switch before play begins.

Diplomat · Moderate

Maste / Bythal

Tok'ra · cultural bridge and team coordinator

Bring people together, keep the team confident, and turn one ally's success into another.

Newly joined, Maste and Bythal carry healing instincts, intelligence experience, and a calling to make peaceful contact across the galaxy.

You are great at

  • First contact, persuasion, and reading motives
  • Inspiring the whole team before danger
  • Culture, medicine, disguise, and recovery scenes

Notice first: Inspire grants temporary HP and unlocks team benefits; Coordinated Fire helps an inspired teammate follow your attack.

Engineer · Moderate

Bervell

Tollan human · field technology specialist

Make impossible technology work long enough for the whole team to get home.

A brilliant Tollan survivor, Bervell refuses to let his people's knowledge vanish. Every broken system is a chance to preserve what they built.

You are great at

  • Repairing mission hardware during a crisis
  • Understanding advanced and alien technology
  • Engineering, science, investigation, and pilot work

Notice first: Advanced Technology helps read high-TL devices; Jury Rig improves repairs; Armorer can strengthen a teammate's gear.

Medic · Moderate

Kulera

Aturen · healer and nonviolent support specialist

Protect life, disappear from danger, and keep the team moving when someone goes down.

Kulera trained in Nox-influenced healing and pacifism. She treats every world as a chance to learn, preserve life, and share care.

You are great at

  • Stabilizing allies and treating exhaustion
  • Understanding plants and biological hazards
  • Finding nonviolent options and calming a crisis

Notice first: Pacifist is a clear character promise; First Aid and Urgent Care improve healing; her dartgun causes exhaustion rather than HP damage.

Scientist · Beginner-friendly

Lanni

Kelownan human · archaeologist and investigator

Ask the question no one else noticed and turn every failed theory into useful momentum.

Lanni is a patient scholar who values the work more than the credit. The Stargate offers cultures, ruins, and mysteries far beyond one planet.

You are great at

  • Interpreting ruins and hidden spaces
  • Investigation, science, culture, nature, and insight
  • Turning failed ideas into the next breakthrough

Notice first: Eureka turns failed Intelligence or Wisdom checks into points that later buy advantage; Archaeologist helps locate hidden rooms.

Open Lanni PDF 4 pages · verified player packet

Scout · Moderate

Oringo

Unas · infiltrator and survival specialist

Go where others cannot, see the danger first, and leave a path the whole team can follow.

Oringo used infiltration and intelligence to help free his people. He now scouts for Phoenix Site while protecting vulnerable Unas and human allies.

You are great at

  • Reconnaissance, tracking, climbing, and safe routes
  • Stealth, perception, survival, and physical resilience
  • Ambush awareness and covert entry

Notice first: Impressive Resilience can resist damage; Survivalist protects against environmental and trap danger; Tracking grants advantage.

Open Oringo PDF 4 pages · verified player packet

Soldier · Moderate

A'tir

Jaffa · defensive anchor and close-range protector

Hold the line with a staff weapon and make everyone beside you harder to hurt.

A'tir turned against a false god and now directs his discipline toward freeing others from Goa'uld rule.

You are great at

  • Protecting clustered allies in dangerous spaces
  • Athletics, intimidation, pilot work, and stealth
  • Heavy armor, Jaffa knowledge, and decisive force

Notice first: Defensive Posture raises nearby teammates' AC; the Ma'Tok has ranged and melee modes; symbiote resilience changes recovery.

Soldier · Pilot · Beginner-friendly

Capt. Selena Rodriguez

Tau'ri human · field leader and pilot

Call the target, take the shot, and help a mixed team move as one under pressure.

Rodriguez is a decorated pilot who excels at bringing people with different histories into one working team.

You are great at

  • Ranged coordination and frontline leadership
  • Pilot, athletics, engineering, culture, and perception
  • Vehicle scenes, first contact, and extraction

Notice first: Assault Coordination strengthens the next teammate's ranged hit; Tactical Flexibility changes tactics; Recovery improves short rests.

Prefer your own hero?

Ask before building.

Premade heroes are the simplest path. If you hope to bring an approved custom character, contact Kyle before the session so the level, origins, equipment, rules version, and mission fit can be confirmed.

A custom character uses one of the seven player seats. Approval is not automatic, and there is no dependable external builder you need to use.
