Before you step inside

A few things
worth knowing.

Can I play if I’ve never tried a tabletop RPG?

Yes. A tabletop role-playing game is a shared story: you say what your character tries, the GM describes what happens, and the rules help when the outcome is uncertain. No book, dice, character creation, acting, or previous Daggerheart experience is required.

Do I need to know the earlier Goldspire adventures?

No. Soulspire’s Secret is the third Goldspire adventure, but it stands on its own. Returning players may recognize connections; everyone gets a complete story in this session.

What is the Dread tower?

A shared wooden-block tower used alongside Daggerheart. Some moments call for a pull; other pulls let you push your luck. The numbered, colored blocks have consequences, and each pull makes the tower less stable. Kyle explains the custom rules during play.

What does the $40 ticket include?

A four-and-a-half-hour private-room game with live performances, music, visual media, and props; a prepared character package; table reference cards; and a physical charm with an adventure-exclusive ability. Your character materials and charm come home with you.

What should I bring?

Bring yourself and enough time to stay until 3:00 PM. A notebook, pencil, or your own dice are optional. Check the Mox listing for current availability and booking terms.
